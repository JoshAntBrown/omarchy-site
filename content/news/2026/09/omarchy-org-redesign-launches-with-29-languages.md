The website for a beautiful, fun Linux distribution ought to be beautiful and fun too. So we've given [Omarchy.org](/) a complete redesign, and we're launching it in 29 languages across 35 editions. A little retro magic, a lot more to explore, and a much bigger welcome to the world.

Start with the logo. Move your mouse over it. Click it. Change the theme. This is a website that wants you to play with your computer! The pixels, colors, and animations carry the spirit of Omarchy straight into the browser. You can get a feel for the place before you've downloaded a single ISO.

Then scroll. There's room to show off the editors, terminals, agents, games, Windows VM, and hardware that make Omarchy useful every day. Browse [community themes](/themes/), find a plugin, see [real workstations](/workstations/), or discover a meetup near you. Meet the [people building it](/teams/) and the [patrons funding it](/patrons/). We finally have a front door with enough room for everything happening inside.

And that front door now speaks your language. Danish, Japanese, Arabic, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, Irish, Urdu, Tamil, and many more. The main site and every news story are translated. The manual is still in English; we'll tackle that next. These are first editions, and corrections from native speakers are very welcome through [the site repository](https://github.com/omacom/omarchy-site).

We have local domains too: [omarchy.dk](https://omarchy.dk), [omarchy.jp](https://omarchy.jp), [omarchy.se](https://omarchy.se), [omarchy.fr](https://omarchy.fr), [omarchy.mx](https://omarchy.mx), and many others. Community members have generously registered more domains and offered to point them our way. The language addresses, like [vi.omarchy.org](https://vi.omarchy.org), [zh.omarchy.org](https://zh.omarchy.org), and [ga.omarchy.org](https://ga.omarchy.org), give those editions a permanent home while we connect the local names. Use the globe beside the theme picker to jump between them.

This redesign grew out of a wonderful burst of work in the Omarchy Design group. **[Barış Girişmen](https://x.com/BarisGirismen)** created the design that became the foundation of the new site, built the pixel-grid shader, and drove an enormous amount of the implementation and polish. **[Christoffer Hallas](https://x.com/hicsfh)** brought prototypes, animation work, theme transitions, and a steady stream of refinements. **[Daniel Schmier](https://x.com/captainscorch)** contributed designs and implementation for the logo, news, install, team, foundation, and testimonial sections.

**[Andrés Villagrán](https://x.com/avillagran)** brought experiments with audio-reactive effects, theme integration, and the meetup feed, as well as a strong push for translations. **[Ryan Hughes](https://x.com/ryanrhughes)** helped shape the site through detailed feedback and brought the redesign into Astro. **Yam Catzenelson** contributed sound and video experiments, and **Eric Carmichael** worked on the early CRT version.

Thanks as well to **Wes Oudshoorn, Taha, Aloïs Deniel, Adrian Rangel, Niklas Jul, HANCORE, OldJobobo, and Rick Blalock** for the concepts, experiments, ideas, and feedback shared along the way. And to everyone helping secure local domains and welcome people in their own language. It's been a joy watching all this come together.

Go have a look. Pick a theme. Poke the pixels. Computers should be fun!
